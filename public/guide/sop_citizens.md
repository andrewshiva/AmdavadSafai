# Standard Operating Procedure (SOP): Using the AmdavadSafai Platform

Welcome to **AmdavadSafai**, the crowdsourced civic cleanliness platform and real-time municipal accountability network for Ahmedabad. This platform empowers citizens to report uncollected waste, track municipal sanitation resolution, hold elected representatives accountable, and participate in community cleanup drives.

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines all current Version 2 features and workflows.

---

## 1. Platform Navigation & Trilingual Language Selection

AmdavadSafai supports 3 official languages with native typography:

* **English (`EN`)**: Powered by clean, modern geometric typography (*Outfit*).
* **Gujarati (`ગુજ`)**: Powered by native script (*Noto Sans Gujarati*).
* **Hindi (`હિં`)**: Powered by Devanagari script (*Noto Sans Devanagari*).

### Navigation Architecture

The top navigation bar provides access to 6 specialized civic views:

1. **ABOUT US**: Interactive platform introduction, 2-minute walkthrough video with chapter markers, 4-step workflow, Citizen Charter resolution SLAs, and municipal leadership governance.
2. **DASHBOARD**: Real-time GIS heatmap of city-wide reports, intensity scale (Low to Critical), scrolling live municipal feed, and resolution completion metrics.
3. **REPORTS**: Personal and community complaint tracking with status filters (*All*, *Pending*, *In Progress*, *Resolved*).
4. **WARDS**: Comprehensive profiles for all 48 Ahmedabad wards, featuring elected Corporators, MLAs, MPs, active garbage truck fleet counts, resolution performance ratings, and CSV report export.
5. **DRIVES (5)**: Scheduled Sunday community cleanup campaigns with RSVP booking, volunteer headcounts, and designated meeting spots.
6. **IMPACT**: Verified Before & After visual transformation gallery documenting cleaned hotspots across the city.

## 3. Reporting a Civic Issue (4-Step Workflow)

When you spot roadside waste, overflowing dumpsters, sewage spillage, or fallen debris:

### Step 1: Capture & GPS Geotag

1. Click the **"+ REPORT AN ISSUE"** button in the header or on the hero screen.
2. Allow location access: The app auto-detects your coordinates and matches them to one of Ahmedabad's 48 wards.
3. Alternatively, click anywhere on the interactive map to position the pin accurately.
4. Upload clear photographic evidence of the sanitation issue.

### Step 2: AI Categorization & Departmental Routing

Select the appropriate category and severity:

* **Categories**: *Roadside Garbage*, *Overflowing Public Bin*, *Construction Debris (Malba)*, *Drainage/Sewage Leak*, *Dead Animal Removal*, *Street Sweeping*.
* **Severity**: *Minor*, *Moderate*, *Severe*, *Critical*.
* The platform automatically routes the ticket to the respective AMC department:
  * **SWM**: Solid Waste Management Department
  * **Drainage & Sewage**: Engineering Division
  * **CNCD**: Cattle Network & Animal Control
  * **Electrical**: Street Lighting Division

### Step 3: Real-Time SLA Tracking

* Every submitted complaint receives an official **AMC CCRS Ticket ID** (e.g., `AS-311-2026`).
* The system monitors progress against the **Citizen Charter SLA**:
  * **Garbage & Debris**: 12-hour resolution target.
  * **Dead Animal Removal**: 24-hour resolution target.
  * **Drainage Blockage**: 48-hour resolution target.

### Step 4: Verification & Karma Reward

* Once municipal teams complete the cleanup, the supervisor uploads verified after-photos.
* Inspect the resolution photo and confirm the fix.
* Confirming cleanups awards you **+50 Karma Points**.

## 4. Ward Profiles, Elected Representatives & CSV Reports

Access the **WARDS** view to inspect your local administrative unit:

1. **Select Ward**: Choose your ward from the dropdown (e.g., *Navrangpura*, *Bodakdev*, *Maninagar*, *Ghatlodiya*).
2. **Elected Representatives**:
   * View your **Ward Corporator** name, contact details, and municipal phone lines.
   * View the serving **MLA** (Member of Legislative Assembly) and **MP** (Member of Parliament).
3. **Operational Metrics**:
   * **Active Open Issues**: Unresolved complaints currently under dispatch.
   * **Solved This Month**: Total verified cleanups in the current calendar month.
   * **Active Vehicle Fleet**: Number of AMC hydraulic compactors and tippers deployed.
   * **Resolution Performance Rating**: Percentage compliance with Citizen Charter SLAs.
4. **Download Ward Report**:
   * Click **"WARD REPORT"** to export an official CSV summary containing ward health metrics, representative details, and open issues for civic audits or community meetings.

## 5. Sunday Cleanup Drives (Volunteer Network)

Participate directly in community transformation via the **DRIVES** view:

* **Upcoming Events**: View upcoming Sunday cleanliness drives scheduled across major heritage and riverfront zones (e.g., *Sabarmati Riverfront Walk*, *Bhadra Fort Heritage Plaza*, *Kankaria Lake Precinct*).
* **RSVP & Volunteer Count**: Click **RSVP NOW** to register attendance. Real-time counters display registered volunteers.
* **Safety & Equipment**: All registered volunteers receive safety gloves, biodegradable bags, and sanitizers on site.
* **Karma Reward**: Participating in a verified Sunday cleanup awards **+100 Karma Points**.

---

## 6. Impact Gallery (Before & After Proof)

Visit the **IMPACT** view to inspect verified municipal resolutions:

* **Before / After Comparison**: Toggle between the original citizen report photo and the municipal sanitation team's completed cleanup photo.
* **Geotagged Proof**: Every card includes verified ward names, timestamp of resolution, and tonnage of debris removed.
* **Citizen Upvotes**: Community members can endorse verified transformations to highlight high-performing sanitation teams.

---

## 7. Account Management & Civic Profile Dropdown

Click your circular avatar at the top right of the header to open the profile dropdown:

* **Citizen Role**: Displays your registered name and status (*Active Citizen* or *AMC Sanitation Officer*).
* **Monday Digest**: Subscribe to weekly civic health summaries and cleanliness rankings for your ward.
* **Changelog**: Review recent platform updates, bug fixes, and new features.
* **Citizen SOP Guide**: Access this guide at any time with trilingual language switching.
* **Sign Out**: Safely sign out of your session.

---

## 8. Emergency Escalation Channels

For urgent civic hazards requiring immediate municipal intervention:

* **AMC Toll-Free Central Helpline**: Dial `155303` (24/7 Central Control Room).
* **Official Municipal Email**: `swm@ahmedabadcity.gov.in`
* **Direct Escalation**: Use the *Call AMC Helpline* button on any ward profile to connect directly to the zonal control room.
